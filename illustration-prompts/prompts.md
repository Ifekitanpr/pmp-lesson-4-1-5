# Lesson 4.1.5 illustration prompts

Shared style for every asset: flat story-set editorial illustration with recognizable human characters and a concrete project-management situation; simple, professional, meaningful, non-abstract; pure white background; no border, container, shadow, gradient, text, letters, numbers, or watermark. Palette: #007BFF, #0F172A, #FF6B35, #94A3B8, and white. Landscape 3:2.

1. `hero-conductor-disconnected.png` — A conductor at a podium faces five musicians who are rehearsing independently with visibly disconnected timing and attention.
2. `modal-conductor-integration.png` — A conductor coordinates five musicians into one coherent performance, with subtle visual paths connecting podium and players.
3. `plans-to-one-system.png` — A project manager gathers five distinct planning artifacts into one organized integrated project folder.
4. `modal-consistency-review.png` — A project manager and domain leads compare separate plans, find conflicting assumptions, and align them.
5. `vertical-integration-card.png` — A project manager connects a subsidiary risk plan upward to objectives, governance, and a business case.
6. `horizontal-integration-card.png` — Project leads align scope, calendar, budget, staffing, and risk plans side by side.
7. `modal-vertical-quiz.png` — A risk lead's escalation trigger is visibly disconnected from the governance framework above.
8. `living-plan.png` — A team progressively elaborates a plan: detailed near-term work and lightly sketched future work.
9. `modal-progressive-elaboration.png` — New information, an approved baseline, and stakeholder feedback are incorporated into an integrated plan.
10. `modal-living-plan-quiz.png` — A PM marks a plan complete in month one while later risks, changes, and feedback arrive unattended.
11. `conductor-connected.png` — A conductor coordinates a full orchestra in one unified performance.
12. `modal-integrator-summary.png` — A PM at the center aligns upward governance connections and horizontal domain connections.
